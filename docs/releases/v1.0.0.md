## Camera and iceoryx

The packaging dependency guard preserves the iceoryx2 v0.8.1 build fix. The final native runtime additionally retains view state for RGB scene captures and selects the project's GI/reflection methods, allowing Warehouse indirect lighting into the fisheye capture. This does not change segmentation captures or the shared-memory transport.

The final runtime delivered 242 valid 640x480 BGRA8 raw camera messages in 30 seconds (145 distinct timestamps), 698 cadence messages and 6001 IMU messages. Logs confirm successful iceoryx publishes with a recipient. No RPC camera bypass was used. See `lighting-frames.json`, `lighting-runtime.log` and `lighting-camera.jpg`.

The Warehouse viewport renders clearly. The final fisheye image shows the shelves, floor and roof, but some patterned roof/wrapped surfaces remain. This baseline fixes loading, image publication and dark scene-capture lighting; it does not claim artifact-free rendering or validate every other pack.
